# AI Approval Checklist

## Governance

- [ ] Business Owner named
- [ ] AI Risk Owner named
- [ ] Preliminary risk tier confirmed
- [ ] Required governance body identified
- [ ] Risk register entry created

## Data and privacy

- [ ] Data provenance documented
- [ ] Data quality/representativeness assessed where required
- [ ] Least-privilege access confirmed
- [ ] Retention requirements documented
- [ ] Privacy/Legal review completed where required
- [ ] Sensitive-data use explicitly approved where applicable

## Model/system

- [ ] Intended use and limitations documented
- [ ] Functional validation completed
- [ ] Fairness testing completed where required
- [ ] Adversarial/security testing completed where required
- [ ] Version/configuration tracking established
- [ ] Monitoring baseline defined

## Human oversight

- [ ] Human review is available where mandatory
- [ ] Human can meaningfully override/intervene
- [ ] Escalation route tested
- [ ] Customer contestability route defined where applicable

## Third party

- [ ] Vendor due diligence completed
- [ ] Relevant certifications/evidence reviewed
- [ ] Vendor evidence not treated as a substitute for Northstar testing
- [ ] Subprocessors and data handling reviewed
