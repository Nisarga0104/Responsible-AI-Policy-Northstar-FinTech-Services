# Responsible AI Policy — Northstar FinTech Services

**AI Governance Portfolio — Project 3 of 5**  
**Prepared by:** Nisarg Kamble  
**Organization:** Northstar FinTech Services Pvt. Ltd. *(fictional, portfolio use)*  
**Date:** September 2026

## Overview

This project converts responsible-AI principles into an operational enterprise policy for a fictional fintech organization.

The policy covers AI systems that are developed internally or procured from third parties, including customer-service AI, fraud detection, marketing personalization, employee productivity AI, document processing, and systems that inform credit-related decisions.

The policy is designed to work alongside the NIST AI RMF-based risk assessment in Project 2 rather than replace it.

## What this repository demonstrates

- Responsible AI policy design
- AI governance operating model
- Risk-tiered AI approval
- AI lifecycle controls
- Human oversight and contestability
- Data governance and provenance
- AI security and adversarial-risk controls
- Third-party AI governance
- Incident management
- Shadow-AI / unapproved-use controls
- Policy exceptions and enforcement
- Practical governance templates

## Core policy model

**Principles → Roles → Risk Classification → Lifecycle Gates → Controls → Monitoring → Incident Response → Review**

## Important boundary

Northstar is fictional. The risk tiers and governance requirements are internal portfolio constructs and are not presented as legal classifications.

The policy also does not itself establish legal compliance. Applicable law and regulatory requirements require separate review by Legal/Compliance.
